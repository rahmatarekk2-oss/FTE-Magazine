BS Fintech and Entrepreneurship

Inspiring the next generation of digital ventures

Source: KBSBS Fintech & Entrepreneurship04
Overview
  • Prepares entrepreneurial, tech-enabled professionals to design and scale innovative ventures at the intersection of finance, technology, and digital platforms
  • Integrates finance, computing, AI, cybersecurity, and blockchain to build strong capabilities in developing secure, data-driven, and scalable business solutions
  • Develops understanding of crowd behavior, digital marketing, and new media to help students grow platforms, engage users, and drive adoption in competitive digital markets
  • Equips graduates to launch, manage, and expand fintech and technology ventures by combining analytical thinking, innovation, and venture-building skills

ENTR 410

Case Studies in FinTech & Venture Creation

Pre-requisiteENTR 250Topics6
  • Analysing real startup cases to help formulate entrepreneurial strategy.
  • Analysing real startup cases to understand how founders: make high-stakes choices under uncertainty; evaluate customer needs, competitive dynamics, pricing and unit economics; make go-to-market strategies, team challenges, financing options, and pivot decisions; identify pain points in finance and validate whether the problem is real, urgent, and worth solving; and Attract customers when trust is fragile
  • Analysing cases on venture capital valuation, raising capital with limited traction, narrative building, and establishing credibility in pitching
  • Analysing cases involving ethics, risk management, fraud spikes, outages, breaches, and incident response; how leaders balance speed, cost, and resilience.
  • Identifying pain points in legacy finance and validating whether a software or technological solution is real, urgent, and worth solving.
  • Examining FinTech technologies and applications in payments, lending, banking, insurance, and investment services.
